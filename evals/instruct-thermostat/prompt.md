Write the user instructions for resetting the Wi-Fi on our smart thermostat. The steps: hold the button for 10 seconds until the light blinks blue, open the app, tap Devices > Thermostat > Reset Wi-Fi, choose the network, enter the password, wait for the light to turn green. If it blinks red, the password was wrong.

Save the finished piece to draft.md in the current directory.
