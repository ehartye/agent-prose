# Professional presentation and webinar research

## Coverage and decision

Existing speech support times narration; the presentation file tool builds decks. Add two planning skills for the audience argument and for event production, keeping actual file and platform operations in their existing tools. Five research lenses covered academic, technical, applied, current platform behavior and contrary evidence. Seven new sources were captured; the existing WAI checklist was reused. Sources below were checked on 2026-10-07.

| Source | Contribution | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| [Penn State authors: slide research](https://www.writing.engr.psu.edu/research.html) | Same narration with assertion-evidence slides improved understanding/retention in a technical-subject comparison | Author research summary; does not isolate sentence headlines or establish general effectiveness for every audience |
| [Fenesi et al., 2015](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2015.01076/full) | Different groups benefited from different text/image designs | One instructional topic and selected populations; counters an absolute redundant-text ban |
| [WAI: making events accessible](https://www.w3.org/WAI/teach-advocate/accessible-presentations/) | Accessible advance materials, visual descriptions, readable slides and activity considerations | Qualitative guidance; no certified speech rate or guaranteed conformance |
| [GSA: accessible meetings](https://www.section508.gov/create/accessible-meetings/) | Accommodation routes, accessible participation, rehearsal of accommodations and accessible recordings | Federal-agency legal context; practical advice does not determine legal duties for every event |
| [Stanford: virtual event roles and troubleshooting](https://osep.stanford.edu/planning/virtual-and-hybrid-event-planning/virtual-event-team-roles-presenter-tips-and) | Host/backup/moderation/time/support ownership, soundcheck and communicated fallback | Practitioner guidance; generic backup advice must be reconciled with real platform permissions |
| [Zoom: practice sessions](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0062566) | Deliberate backstage/live transition; automatic computer recording may begin in practice | Product/account-specific behavior; practice does not mean unrecorded |
| [Zoom: webinar roles](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0065551) | Co-host versus alternative-host start permissions and configurable panelist permissions | Actual account and licensing must be checked |
| [Microsoft: attendance and engagement reports](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/teams-analytics-and-reports/meeting-attendance-report) | Logged actions/intervals and policy exclusions explain incomplete reporting populations | Participation is observable; comprehension, attention and business impact require other evidence |

## What remains convention

The slide authoring fields, 45-minute sample run sheet, 12-minute sample decision allocation, bounded demo wait and cut-order procedure are maintainer recommendations. They are not tested universal numerical rules. A word-rate budget estimates narration; a timed rehearsal checks a complete event. The skills do not add automated slide-quality or accessibility certification.

## Baseline and evaluation

The [initial baseline outputs](2026-10-07-professional-event-baseline.md) correctly rejected unsupported performance claims and unsupported ROI. The targeted gaps were accessible materials/participation, captions and recording checks, rehearsal cuts and metric definitions. The [design](../superpowers/specs/2026-10-07-professional-events-design.md) fixes the behavioral rubric before the paired round. Results and independently checked limitations are recorded in the verification report.
