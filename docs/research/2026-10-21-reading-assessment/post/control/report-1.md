Prioritize these changes without rewriting the passage yet:

1. Divide P1 into a study setup, the two-group comparison, and a causal qualification. Keep the six-week duration and one-school scope. Keep 68 percent for students reporting at least eight hours of sleep and 45 percent for students reporting less sleep; retain that homework completion was also reported by students. Parallel group descriptions will make the comparison easier to follow.
2. Retain and define **association** at first use: for example, “a pattern linking the reported sleep and homework results.” Retain **self-report** and its existing explanation. Repeated “reported” wording carries evidence about the measurement method; removing it for smoothness would change the meaning.
3. Replace peripheral formal language locally: “previous school performance” for “prior attainment,” and “responsibilities outside school” for “obligations outside school.” Explain that students could have differed in these ways, so the survey cannot separate sleep's possible effect from other possible influences. Do not assert that any particular alternative actually caused the difference.
4. Make the causal limitation direct: “This survey alone cannot show that more sleep caused students to finish their homework.” Preserve P3's distinction between lack of causal proof and proof of no effect.
