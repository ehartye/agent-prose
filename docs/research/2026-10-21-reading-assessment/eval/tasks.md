# Passage assessment tasks

Answer all three requests independently. Each passage is original fictional evaluation material, not evidence about an actual study or historical event. Preserve the supplied source files. Write a report for each task, not a replacement passage. Suggested edits may be quoted locally.

## Task 1

Is `source-1.md` suitable for an average U.S. high school freshman reading independently? Give a rubric-like rating and propose changes. Assume students can compare percentages but have not studied research design. Do not rewrite the passage yet. Keep association, self-report, both percentages, the study duration, and the causal limitation.

## Task 2

Assess `source-2.md` for grade 7 students in a teacher-led literature lesson. The task is to infer what the narrator knows and cite evidence. Give criterion ratings and suggested changes or supports. Keep the uncertainty and viewpoint work that make the task useful; do not assume a complex sentence is automatically a defect.

## Task 3

Rate `source-3.md` for grade 6 independent reading and propose changes. These readers have learned about floating and sinking, but not particle models. Explain what a numerical readability estimate can and cannot establish here. Do not add a factual explanation that is unsupported by the passage.
