# Original fictional scene

P1. By the time the bell rang, the blue envelope was gone. I had left it under the chipped cup, where no one would look unless they knew why the cup stood on the windowsill instead of the shelf. Mara said the wind must have taken it. Yet the cup had not moved, and the window, which she always insisted on leaving open, was latched.

P2. I told her it did not matter. She smiled then, or perhaps I only remember a smile because of what happened later. At twelve, when the road was still bright and the kitchen already dark, I put the cup back on the shelf. I did not ask Mara to help me search.
